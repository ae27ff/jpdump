DNL continuation report: not confirmed for JPEG T.81. Section B.2.5 requires
DNL at the end of the first scan. Entropy data after DNL without another SOS
is not a valid JPEG scan. A legal DNL-at-scan-end regression passes and resolves
the initially zero SOF height. A continuation without SOS is rejected.
Reference: https://www.w3.org/Graphics/JPEG/itu-t81.pdf
