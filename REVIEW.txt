Review/fix loop follow-up (2026-10-04)

Repeated complete source reviews, focused reproductions, fixes, and regression
checks found and fixed the following additional issues. The final source pass

16 Shared loop globals could skip or repeat headers, including an infinite
   rendering loop when EXIF byte-array conversion reset the outer loop index.
   All affected helpers and rendering loops now use local variables. Rendering
   tests exercise the actual accordion code, including cross-realm arrays.
17 Uploads retained FileReader debug references and used a nonstandard binary
   string API. ArrayBuffer reads and bounded byte-to-string conversion now
   preserve binary bytes. Read failures clear stale previews/results/downloads;
   failures from older selections cannot replace the current selection.
18 Metadata object rendering could inject markup or throw for a field named
   hasOwnProperty. It now escapes values and uses a safe own-property check.
   Accordion buttons use text headings and cannot submit an enclosing form.
19 The displayed dimensions depended on browser codec support. They now come
   from parsed frame/SIZ/DHP data. JPEG-LS scan fields and mapping selectors,
   lossless frame names, and JPEG 2000 POC/COM/EOC marker labels are corrected.
   Incomplete/unrecognized markers have defined labels instead of undefined.
20 A malformed known EXIF value or optional IFD/thumbnail pointer could discard
   otherwise valid tags. Counts, types, offsets and byte ranges are checked per
   field/directory within the APP1 boundary. ASCII requires its terminating NUL.
21 Signed byte/short and IEEE float/double TIFF fields were silently omitted.
   Their values now use the declared type and byte order. Unknown enumeration
   values remain raw; inherited lookup properties cannot become fake values.
22 Empty/unusable EXIF directories could hide later valid APP1 metadata. Such
   directories are skipped. Legal marker fill preceding SOI is also accepted
   by the shared EXIF/IPTC/XMP segment traversal.
23 XML names such as __proto__, constructor and toString could disappear or
   corrupt the conversion result. Conversion maps now have no object prototype.
24 Whitespace-only XML literals and inherited xml:space="preserve" text were
   lost. Leaf text and explicit whitespace preservation now survive conversion.
25 A legitimate parsererror element was mistaken for a parser failure, and
   XMLDocument implementations without children lost the XMP root. Parser-error
   detection now checks its namespace; root conversion uses documentElement.
26 Scan parameters, selector bounds/order, interleaved sampling totals, repeated
   or incomplete components, and progressive DC/refinement history could be
   reported valid despite malformed structure. These are now checked per frame.
27 Restart markers could appear after a closed scan, with no restart interval,
   or in the wrong sequence. Active-scan state, interval widths and modulo-eight
   ordering are now checked. Empty entropy segments are rejected.
28 Truncated/invalid DQT, DHT and DAC definitions were accepted. Payload sizes,
   table classes/destinations, nonzero quantizers, Huffman tree capacity and
   arithmetic conditioning ranges are checked. Eight-bit scans cannot use a
   sixteen-bit quantizer; progressive scans cannot change a used quantizer.
29 Multiple/differential frames could be accepted without a hierarchy or valid
   references. DHP parameters, component coverage/references, coding-family
   consistency, differential predictors and EXP placement are now checked.
30 Valid JPEG-LS extended DNL/LSE dimensions were misread, while malformed LSE
   presets/interleave modes were accepted. Declared widths, dimensions, NEAR,
   thresholds (including defaults) and RESET bounds are now checked.
31 JPEG 2000 SIZ/default/tile-part structure was largely unchecked. Mandatory
   headers, geometry, components, tile IDs/parts, Psot bounds and SOP lengths are
   checked. Psot starts at the actual SOT marker after fill bytes; reserved
   no-length markers and unknown length-bearing segments remain inspectable.
32 An unused helper replaced native String.replaceAll with incompatible regular
   expression behavior. The replacement was removed.

Three-way review and fixes (2026-10-04)

Participants: the primary Codex reviewer, GPT-5.6 Sol, and GPT-6 Sol. Each reviewed
the complete source and tests independently over repeated passes. Candidates were
reproduced and checked against the published specifications before changes were
accepted. All three agree with the final implementation. The final complete pass
found no further actionable correctness bugs within this inspector's scope.

Additional agreed changes:
- Nested accordions use natural height so expanded metadata and wrapped text are
  visible. Independent collapsing remains supported; fixed-height animation is
  replaced by immediate expansion. Verified in the real browser.
- EXIF DigitalZoomRatio has its standard name; EXIF identification requires the
  full six-byte signature. JPEG 2000 CRG and recovered EOC markers have defined
  labels. The upload filter includes the supported .jls/image/jls format.
- Classic JPEG reserved marker codes are flagged. DNL has its exact two-byte
  payload; JPEG-LS retains its wider line counts. EXP with no expansion is flagged.
- JPEG-LS LSE cannot change a nonzero declared dimension or resolve unknown
  height too late. Legal subsampling is accepted; sample-interleaved components
  must have equal actual extents, including after DNL resolves height. Component
  ordering, marker families, mapping entry widths/counts, adjacent continuations,
  and point-transform/mapping compatibility are checked. External abbreviated
  mapping tables and mapping-table-only streams remain supported.
- JPEG 2000 COD/COC fields, precincts, QCD/QCC steps, component overrides and
  precedence are checked. Quantizers match reversible/irreversible transforms in
  both directions. MCT requires compatible precision, separation and wavelets.
  Shared tile defaults are cached to avoid repeated component validation.
- Known JPEG 2000 header placement, multiplicity and tile-part restrictions are
  checked. COM/CRG/POC and TLM/PLM/PLT/PPM/PPT bodies are checked with indexed
  fragment ordering, legal continuations and PPM/PPT exclusion. TLM entries match
  actual tile parts and Psot; PLM/PPM group counts match tile parts. Pointer data
  stays compact instead of allocating an object for every length entry.
- Invalid input is inspected where trustworthy marker boundaries remain. Later
  headers are shown as recovered/unverified, and incomplete segments retain their
  declared length, available raw bytes and complete SOF/SOS fields. Incorrect
  JPEG 2000 Psot values do not hide a safely located EOC. Unknown boundaries stop
  traversal without guessing markers inside payloads. The UI shows warnings and
  distinguishes invalid structure, truncation, recovery and unparsed data.
- Truncated APP1 segments can supply complete EXIF fields through a bounded
  reader and still show the EXIF accordion. Truncated APP13 IPTC resources retain
  earlier complete datasets and tolerate missing final padding. No new segment or
  resource boundary is inferred beyond the available data.

Reconciled specification questions:
- An earlier DNL length interpretation was based on merged PDF text rows. Direct
  inspection of the published T.81 table confirms Ld=4. JPEG-LS extends this.
- The published T.87 allows DNL to redefine height; a draft's stricter rule was
  not applied. Its prohibition on changing nonzero dimensions concerns LSE.
- T.800 permits retained extra quantizer steps after subband truncation and POC
  in later tile parts when first-part POC exists. Generic POC field bounds were
  preserved without inventing additional component/layer limits.
- T.800 A.1.3 requires unassigned bits to be zero, while a decoder ignores them.
  Nonzero reversible-quantizer low bits and unassigned Stlm bits are flagged but
  their data remains available. Table A.32 explicitly defines CEpoc=0 as 256 for
  both field widths. PLM minimum Lplm is 4, and an Nplm group may start in one
  marker and continue in another without interrupting a packet-length number.
- Indexed pointer fragments need not be physically ordered or have contiguous
  index values. They are concatenated in numeric order. T.81 B.5's subsection
  list identifies DQT/DHT/COM/APP; it does not authorize DRI/DNL-only streams.
